Scholarships for Pakistani Students Abroad 2025 — Complete List

By NextStep International·May 29, 2026·8 min read
#Scholarships#Pakistani Students#Study Abroad#Free Education#2025

Every Pakistani student wants to know: can I study abroad for free or at reduced cost? The answer is yes — if you know where to look. In 2025, there are dozens of scholarship programs specifically available to Pakistani students wanting to study in Georgia, Azerbaijan, Turkey, China, and beyond.

This guide covers every major scholarship opportunity, eligibility requirements, amounts, and how to apply.

💡 Reality Check: Full free scholarships are competitive and limited. However, partial scholarships and university fee waivers of 20–50% are much more accessible — especially for MBBS and Engineering students with good grades.

Government Scholarships (Pakistan-Backed)

🏛️ HEC (Higher Education Commission) Overseas Scholarship

Fully Funded Available Masters & PhD HEC Pakistan

HEC offers fully-funded scholarships for Masters and PhD students to study in top universities worldwide. Covers tuition, living allowance, airfare and health insurance.

  • Who: Pakistani nationals with minimum 16-year education
  • Countries: Multiple — USA, UK, Europe, China, and more
  • Apply: hec.gov.pk (Check for annual intake)

🇨🇳 Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

Fully Funded Undergraduate to PhD China

The Chinese Government Scholarship is one of the most popular options for Pakistani students. Covers full tuition, accommodation, monthly stipend and health insurance.

  • Programs: MBBS, Engineering, Business, IT — all available
  • Medium: English or Chinese (depends on university)
  • Deadline: February–April each year
  • Apply via: csc.edu.cn or through Pakistani universities

🇹🇷 Türkiye Scholarships (YTB)

Fully Funded All Levels Turkey

Turkey offers fully-funded scholarships to international students including Pakistanis. Very popular among Pakistani students for Business, Law, and Social Sciences.

  • Covers: Tuition + monthly stipend + accommodation + airfare + health insurance
  • Deadline: January–February each year
  • Apply via: turkiyeburslari.gov.tr
  • Language: Turkish (1-year language course provided)

Azerbaijan Scholarship for Pakistani Students

🇦🇿 Azerbaijan State Scholarship

Partial Funded Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan offers bilateral scholarships under Pakistan-Azerbaijan education agreements. These cover partial tuition for medical and engineering students.

  • Limited seats — highly competitive
  • Apply via Ministry of Education Pakistan or through universities
  • Check edu.gov.az for current openings

How to Maximise Your Chances of Getting a Scholarship

  1. Apply early — Most scholarships have February–April deadlines
  2. Keep your FSc/A-Level grades above 75–80% — Minimum requirement for most
  3. Write a strong personal statement — Explain your goals clearly
  4. Get HEC-attested documents ready — Required for most government scholarships
  5. Apply to multiple scholarships simultaneously — Do not put all eggs in one basket

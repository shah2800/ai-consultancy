💡 Reality: Georgia is significantly cheaper than Western Europe. A Pakistani student can live comfortably in Tbilisi for $300–400/month including everything. This is much less than the UK ($1,500+) or USA ($1,800+).

Food in Georgia — Is Halal Available?

This is the most common question Pakistani students ask. The answer is yes — halal food is easily available in Tbilisi, especially in areas with large Muslim student populations.

  • 🕌 There are several mosques in Tbilisi, mostly in the old city area
  • 🥩 Halal butcher shops are found near university areas and in the old town
  • 🍖 Pakistani and Indian restaurants are increasingly available in Tbilisi
  • 🍕 Georgian food itself features many naturally halal options (bread, cheese, vegetables, grilled meats)
  • 🛒 Most supermarkets label imported halal products

Outside Tbilisi (in cities like Batumi or Kutaisi), halal options are more limited — something to factor in when choosing your university location.

Safety in Georgia

Georgia is consistently ranked as one of the safest countries for students. Pakistani students generally report feeling very safe.

  • ✅ Georgia ranks in the top 20 safest countries globally (Numbeo Crime Index)
  • ✅ Very low petty crime — pickpocketing is rare compared to other European cities
  • ✅ Georgian people are known for being hospitable to foreigners
  • ✅ Large Pakistani student community — you will not be alone
  • ⚠️ Like anywhere, use common sense at night in unfamiliar areas
  • ⚠️ Road safety: be careful crossing streets — traffic can be unpredictable

Pakistani Student Community in Georgia

You will not be isolated. There is a large and growing Pakistani student community in Georgia, especially in Tbilisi.

  • Thousands of Pakistani students are currently studying in Georgia
  • Active WhatsApp groups connect students from all major universities
  • Pakistan Student Associations (PSAs) exist at most major universities
  • Students help new arrivals find accommodation, SIM cards, and navigate the city
  • Eid celebrations and Pakistani cultural events are held regularly

Accommodation Options

  • University Hostels: $80–120/month. Basic but convenient. Usually on or near campus.
  • Shared Apartments: $100–180/month per person. Most popular choice — more comfortable.
  • Private Room: $150–250/month. More privacy but higher cost.
  • Homestay: $150–200/month including meals. Good for language learning.

Honest Things to Prepare For

  • 🗣️ Language barrier: Many older Georgians speak only Georgian or Russian. Younger people and university staff speak English. Google Translate is your friend.
  • 🏥 Healthcare: Good hospitals are available in Tbilisi. Private clinics are affordable ($20–40 per consultation). Get health insurance.
  • 💸 Currency: Georgian Lari (GEL). 1 USD ≈ 2.7 GEL. ATMs are everywhere. Wise or Remitly for money transfers from Pakistan.
  • 📶 Internet: Excellent in Tbilisi — fast and cheap. Magti and Geocell are main providers.
  • ✈️ Flights: Tbilisi International Airport has connections via Istanbul (Turkish Airlines) and Dubai (flydubai, Air Arabia). Flight time from Pakistan is 4–6 hours.

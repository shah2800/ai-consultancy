Student Visa Guide for Pakistani Students Going Abroad — 2025

By NextStep International·May 29, 2026·7 min read
#Visa Guide#Student Visa#Georgia#Pakistani Students#Study Abroad

Getting a student visa is the step every Pakistani student worries about most — but it is much simpler than you think. If your documents are correct and your admission is confirmed, the visa process is straightforward for most countries we place students in.

This guide covers the student visa process for Georgia, Azerbaijan, Russia, and Turkey — the most popular destinations for Pakistani students in 2025.

💡 Good News: Georgia offers visa-on-arrival to Pakistani passport holders for short stays. For studying, you apply for a student residence permit after arrival — making it the easiest country to start your education journey.

Georgia Student Visa / Residence Permit

Georgia is the easiest and fastest option for Pakistani students. Here is how it works:

1

Get Admission Letter

Apply to your chosen Georgian university and receive an official admission/offer letter. NextStep International handles this for you.

2

Travel to Georgia

Pakistani passport holders get visa-on-arrival for 90 days. You fly to Tbilisi directly or via Istanbul/Dubai.

3

Register at University

Complete enrollment at your university within 30 days of arrival. The university submits your student status to Georgian authorities.

4

Apply for Residence Permit

Submit your application at the Civil Registry Agency (CRA) in Tbilisi. You get a 1-year student residence permit, renewable annually.

5

Receive Permit Card

Usually issued within 10–30 working days. Cost is approximately GEL 200–400 (around $70–150 USD).

Documents Required for Georgia Student Residence Permit

  • ✅ Valid Pakistani passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • ✅ University admission/enrollment letter
  • ✅ Passport-size photographs (white background)
  • ✅ Proof of accommodation (hostel agreement or rental contract)
  • ✅ Bank statement or proof of financial means
  • ✅ Medical insurance certificate
  • ✅ Application form (provided by CRA)

⚠️ Important: Documents from Pakistan must be attested by HEC (for degrees), notarised, and some require Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation. Our team guides you through this step-by-step.

Azerbaijan Student Visa Process

Azerbaijan requires a pre-arrival visa for Pakistani students. You must obtain a student visa from the Azerbaijan Embassy in Islamabad before travel.

  • Apply at Azerbaijan Embassy, Islamabad or via ASAN Visa (e-visa portal)
  • Submit: passport, admission letter, bank statement, photographs
  • Processing time: 5–10 working days
  • Cost: approximately $50–80 USD
  • Student visa is extended annually through your university

Russia Student Visa Process

Russia has a formal student visa process through the Russian Embassy in Islamabad:

  • University sends an official invitation letter (takes 2–4 weeks)
  • Submit invitation letter + passport + photos at Russian Embassy
  • Processing time: 2–4 weeks
  • Single-entry student visa, converted to multi-entry once in Russia
  • Must register with local police within 3 days of arrival

5 Common Visa Mistakes Pakistani Students Make

  1. Expired or damaged passport — Renew your passport 6+ months before travel
  2. Missing HEC attestation — All degrees must be HEC attested before visa submission
  3. Insufficient bank balance — Most countries require 3–6 months of living expenses shown
  4. Wrong photograph format — Each country has different photo size requirements
  5. Applying too late — Start visa process 2–3 months before your planned departure

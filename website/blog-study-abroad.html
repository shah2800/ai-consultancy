Thousands of Pakistani students study abroad every year — and the number is growing fast. If you're thinking "I want to go abroad for studies but don't know where to start," this guide is for you.

We'll walk you through everything: which country to choose, how to apply, what documents you need, and how a good consultancy can make the whole process easy and affordable.

Step 2 — Choose Your Program

Before applying, decide what you want to study. The most in-demand programs for Pakistani students abroad are:

  • 🏥 MBBS / Medicine — Most popular. Georgia, Azerbaijan and Russia are top choices.
  • 💻 Computer Science / IT — High demand globally. Georgia and Turkey offer great programs.
  • 📊 Business / MBA — Available in almost every country.
  • ⚙️ Engineering — Russia, China and Georgia have strong engineering programs.
  • ⚖️ Law — Georgia and Turkey offer English-taught law degrees.

Step 3 — Check Your Eligibility

Most universities abroad accept Pakistani students with these basic requirements:

  • ✅ FSc / A-Levels or equivalent qualification
  • ✅ 50–60% marks (varies by university)
  • ✅ Valid Pakistani passport
  • ✅ Age 17+ at time of admission
  • ✅ IELTS — only needed for UK, Australia, Canada and some European universities

💡 No IELTS? Don't worry. Georgia, Azerbaijan, Russia, China and Kazakhstan do NOT require IELTS for most programs. English-medium FSc is accepted.

Step 4 — Prepare Your Documents

Common documents required for abroad study applications:

  • Matric and FSc certificates + mark sheets
  • Valid passport (min. 1 year validity)
  • Passport size photographs (white background)
  • Character certificate
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Bank statement (for visa, showing funds)
  • IELTS result (if required by the country)

Common Questions from Pakistani Students

Can I study abroad without IELTS from Pakistan?

Yes. Georgia, Azerbaijan, Russia, China and Kazakhstan do not require IELTS. These are the most popular destinations for Pakistani students in 2025.

How much does it cost to study abroad from Pakistan?

Depends on the country. Georgia and Azerbaijan are cheapest — around $4,000–$8,000 per year tuition, and $200–$400/month living cost. Total 4–6 year cost is roughly $25,000–$55,000.

Is study abroad worth it for Pakistani students?

Absolutely. An international degree opens doors to better jobs, higher salary, and opportunities to work abroad. Many students also find it far cheaper than private universities in Pakistan.

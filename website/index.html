FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers we give every student. For your specific situation, contact us directly.

Is IELTS mandatory?
Not always. Several Georgian universities admit students without IELTS using prior academic English or internal assessment. We match you to programmes where your profile fits — no IELTS shouldn't stop you.
Can I work while studying?
Part-time work may be possible under Georgia's local regulations. Programme intensity — especially in clinical MBBS years — should be considered carefully before committing to work hours.
What are typical semester fees?
Most programmes fall between $1,500 and $3,000 per semester depending on faculty and university. Living costs in Tbilisi are very affordable. Your official offer letter is always the binding document for fees.
Who recognises the degree?
Recognition is determined by national regulators in your home country — not by a consultancy. Georgian medical universities are listed in WHO/FAIMER directories. We always advise families to verify requirements early.

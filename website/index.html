Why Georgia

Georgia offers recognised English-medium programmes, competitive tuition, and a growing international student community — particularly strong for medicine, business, and technology.

Cost effectiveness

Tuition and living costs are typically lower than in many Western European countries, with transparent semester fee structures.

Student environment

Major cities host diverse international cohorts, student services, and practical day-to-day infrastructure.

Work alongside study

English-medium options

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers we give in initial consultations. For case-specific guidance, contact us with your documents.

Is IELTS mandatory?
Not always. Several Georgian universities admit students without IELTS, using prior academic English or internal assessment. We place you where your profile fits.
Can I work while studying?
Part-time work may be possible under local rules. Programme intensity — especially in clinical years — should be weighed before committing to work hours.
What are typical semester fees?
Many programmes fall roughly between USD $1,500 and $3,000 per semester depending on faculty and university. Your offer letter is the binding source.
Who recognises the degree in my home country?
Recognition is determined by national regulators and councils — not by a consultancy. We encourage families to verify requirements with official authorities early.

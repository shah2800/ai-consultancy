FAQ

Common questions

Straightforward answers. For your specific situation, contact us directly.

Is IELTS mandatory?
Not always. Several Georgian universities admit students without IELTS via prior academic English or internal assessment. We match you to programmes where your profile fits.
Can I work while studying?
Part-time work may be possible under Georgia's local regulations. Programme intensity — especially in clinical MBBS years — should be carefully considered before committing to work hours.
What are typical semester fees?
Most programmes range from $1,500 to $3,000 per semester depending on faculty and university. Living costs in Tbilisi are very affordable. Your official offer letter is the binding document for fees.
Who recognises the degree?
Recognition is determined by national regulators in your home country. Georgian medical universities are listed in WHO/FAIMER directories. We always advise families to verify requirements early.
How long does the application take?
From submitting your application to receiving an offer letter typically takes 2–4 weeks. Visa processing varies by embassy. We give you a realistic timeline at your first consultation.

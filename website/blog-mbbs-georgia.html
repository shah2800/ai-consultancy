  • ✅ Low cost of living — $200 to $400/month total expenses

MBBS in Georgia Fees for Pakistani Students

The total cost to study MBBS in Georgia includes tuition fees, accommodation, food and personal expenses:

  • Tuition fee: $4,500 – $8,000 per year
  • Accommodation: $100 – $250 per month (shared hostel/apartment)
  • Food: $80 – $150 per month
  • Total monthly living cost: Approx $200 – $400
  • Total 6-year cost: Approx $35,000 – $60,000 (all included)

Compared to private medical colleges in Pakistan (which cost Rs. 1.5 to 3 crore), studying MBBS in Georgia is significantly cheaper.

Eligibility Requirements

To apply for MBBS in Georgia from Pakistan, you need:

  • ✅ FSc Pre-Medical (at least 50–60% marks)
  • ✅ MDCAT score (required for PMC recognition — check specific university requirements)
  • ✅ Valid Pakistani passport
  • ✅ Age 17 or above at time of admission
  • ✅ No IELTS required (English medium FSc is accepted)

Can I Practice in Pakistan After MBBS in Georgia?

Yes — but you must pass the PMC Licensing Examination (PMLE) after returning to Pakistan. This is the same exam required for MBBS graduates from any foreign country. Many students from Georgia successfully pass the PMLE each year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MBBS from Georgia valid in Pakistan?

Yes, provided your university is recognised by WHO and PMC. You will need to pass the PMC Licensing Exam to practice in Pakistan.

Do I need IELTS for MBBS in Georgia?

No. Most universities in Georgia do not require IELTS. English-medium FSc is sufficient.

How much does MBBS in Georgia cost in Pakistani Rupees?

Approximately Rs. 15 lakh to Rs. 25 lakh per year (tuition only), depending on the university. This is much cheaper than private medical colleges in Pakistan.

Is Georgia safe for Pakistani students?

Yes. Georgia is consistently ranked among the safest countries in Europe. There is a large and growing Pakistani student community in Tbilisi.
